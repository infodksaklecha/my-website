Project Finance
Rescuing a ₹340 Crore Industrial Park from Financial Collapse
Sector: Manufacturing & Infrastructure · Central India

A mid-sized industrial infrastructure developer had been rejected by three banks due to a flawed DPR and a DSCR of 0.91. Through a restructured financial model, separated debt tranches, and a consortium lender approach via SIDBI, the project secured a ₹340 Crore term loan within 5 months.

M&A Advisory
Uncovering Hidden Liabilities in a Cross-Border Acquisition
Sector: Specialty Chemicals · India–Europe Deal

An Indian specialty chemicals manufacturer's acquisition of a European distributor was priced at 4.2x EBITDA based on the target's own diligence. Our independent review revealed misclassified liabilities, unprovisioned environmental obligations, and near-expiring contracts — saving ₹47 Crore upfront through an earn-out restructure.

Largest BSE SME IPO of 2025 — Filed Hours Before a Regulatory Window Closed Forever
